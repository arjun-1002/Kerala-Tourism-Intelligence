The 3-Step Automated Commission & Escrow Flow

STEP 1 · VERIFIED LISTING
Data-Backed Due Diligence

The proponent lists their land, homestay, or resort plan. Our ML intelligence tags it with local footfall metrics (2022–2024 growth) and supply gap scores.

STEP 2 · PLEDGE & AUTO-SPLIT
3.0% Platform Fee Collection

When an investor pledges e.g. ₹10,00,000, the payment gateway automatically routes ₹30,000 (3%) to platform revenue and ₹9,70,000 (97%) to the project escrow.

STEP 3 · MILESTONE ESCROW
Milestone Disbursements

Funds are released to the entrepreneur upon verified execution milestones (licenses, civil work), while the investor receives legal partnership certificates and revenue-share payouts.
